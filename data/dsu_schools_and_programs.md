# Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) - Schools & Academic Programs
Source URL: https://www.dsu.edu.in/academics

Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) in Bengaluru, Karnataka offers undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), online, and doctoral (Ph.D.) programs across multiple specialized schools.

## 1. School of Engineering
- **B.Tech Specializations:**
  - Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
  - CSE with Data Science
  - CSE with Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML)
  - Artificial Intelligence & Robotics
  - Cyber Security
  - AI & Data Science
  - Aerospace Engineering
  - Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
  - Mechanical Engineering
  - Civil Engineering
- **M.Tech Specializations:** Computer Science & Engineering, Design Engineering, Embedded Systems, AI & Data Science, AI & Robotics.

## 2. School of Commerce & Management Studies
- **Programs:** BBA, B.Com, and MBA (corporate-aligned specializations).
- **Online Degrees:** BBA, B.Com, and MBA tracks are also offered through DSU Online Degree Programs (dsuonline.com).

## 3. School of Computer Applications
- **Programs:** BCA and MCA (focused on software development, IT skills, and core computing; BCA is also available via DSU Online).

## 4. School of Basic & Applied Sciences
- **Programs:** B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees.
- **Specializations:** Biological Sciences, Data Science, Cyber Security, and core applied science disciplines.

## 5. School of Law
- **Programs:** B.A., LL.B. (Hons.), B.B.A., LL.B. (Hons.), and 3-year LL.B. programs (approved by Bar Council of India).

## 6. School of Health Sciences
Comprises specialized constituent colleges:
- **College of Nursing Sciences:** B.Sc. Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing, M.Sc. Nursing.
- **College of Pharmaceutical Sciences:** B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D.
- **College of Physiotherapy:** BPT, MPT.
- **College of Allied Health Sciences (CAHS):** B.Sc. in Cardiac Care Technology, Medical Imaging & Radiology, Medical Laboratory Technology, Emergency & Trauma Care, Anesthesia & Operation Theater Technology, and Master of Public Health (MPH).

## 7. School of Design & Digital Trans-Media
- **Programs:** B.Design (B.Des) and digital media tracks covering UI/UX, product design, and visual communication.

## 8. Doctoral (Ph.D.) Programs
- Ph.D. programs are offered across Engineering, Management, Basic & Applied Sciences, Health Sciences, and Law.
