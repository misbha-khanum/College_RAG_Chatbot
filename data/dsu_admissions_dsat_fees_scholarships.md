# DSU Admissions, DSAT, Eligibility, Scholarships & Fee Structure
Source URL: https://admissions.dsu.edu.in/

## 1. Admissions & DSAT Overview
- **DSAT (Dayananda Sagar Scholarship Admission Test):** DSU's university-level entrance and scholarship test conducted for admission to UG and PG programs (including B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.A. LL.B. (Hons.), MBA, M.Tech, etc.).
- **Other Accepted Entrance Exams:** KCET, COMEDK UGET, JEE Main, CAT, MAT, CMAT, K-PGCET, and NEET (depending on the program).
- **Application Steps:** Online Registration -> Application Submission -> Document Upload -> Entrance Test (DSAT / National or State Exam) -> Personal Interview / Counseling -> Seat Allotment & Fee Payment.
- **Admissions Helpdesk:** `admissions@dsu.edu.in` | `080 46461800`

## 2. Eligibility Criteria (Key Programs)
- **B.Tech:** Passed 10+2 or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus Chemistry/Biotechnology/Computer Science/Technical Vocational subjects, with at least **45% aggregate marks** (40% for reserved categories).
- **MBA:** Recognized 3-year Bachelor's degree with at least **50% aggregate marks** (45% for reserved categories), plus valid CAT, MAT, CMAT, K-PGCET, or DSAT scores.
- **B.Pharm / Pharm.D:** 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and English along with Biology/Mathematics and prescribed minimum marks.
- **Law (B.A. LL.B. / B.B.A. LL.B.):** 10+2 from a recognized board meeting Bar Council of India (BCI) eligibility standards.

## 3. Approximate Fee Structure
Fees vary by program, specialization, and admission quota (CET vs. Direct / DSAT / JEE Main):
- **B.Tech (CET / Govt Quota):** ~INR 1,27,000 to INR 1,40,000 per year.
- **B.Tech (Direct / JEE Main / DSAT Quota):** ~INR 3,22,000 to INR 5,25,000 per year depending on core vs. specialized branches (CSE / AI-ML specializations are in the higher bracket).
- **MBA (2 years total):** ~INR 12.04 Lakhs total.
- **Other UG Programs (BBA, B.Com, BCA, etc.):** ~INR 92,000 to INR 4,35,000 per year.
- **Other PG Programs (M.Tech, M.Pharm, M.Sc., etc.):** ~INR 1,42,000 to INR 6,02,000 per year.
- **Hostel & Mess Fees:** ~INR 1,61,000 to INR 2,51,400 per year depending on room sharing (2-sharing, 3-sharing, or 4-sharing).

## 4. Scholarships & Financial Aid
- **DSAT Merit Scholarships:** High DSAT scores qualify applicants for merit-based tuition fee concessions.
- **Additional Scholarships:** Merit-cum-means concessions, sports quota scholarships, defense personnel children grants, and sibling scholarships (applied through the DSU administrative financial aid desk).
