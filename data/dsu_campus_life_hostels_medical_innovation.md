# DSU Campus Life, Hostels, Library, Transport, CDSIMER & Innovation Labs
Source URL: https://www.dsu.edu.in/accommodation

## 1. Hostels & Accommodation
- **Availability:** Separate, secure on-campus hostels for men and women across the Main Campus (Harohalli, Kanakapura Road) and City Innovation Campus.
- **Room Types & Amenities:** 2-sharing, 3-sharing, and 4-sharing rooms equipped with beds, study tables, chairs, wardrobes, Wi-Fi, 24/7 CCTV security, recreation/TV rooms, reading spaces, indoor games, and hygienic mess facilities (vegetarian and non-vegetarian options).

## 2. Library & Learning Resources
- Centralized and school-specific constituent libraries across Engineering, Sciences, Health Sciences, Law, and Management.
- Extensive print textbooks, reference volumes, national/international journals, digital e-resources/databases, anti-plagiarism tools, and quiet reading spaces.

## 3. Campus Transport
- Dedicated university bus/transport network connecting major parts of Bengaluru city and surrounding areas to both the Harohalli Main Campus and City Innovation Campus for day scholars and staff.

## 4. Medical Facilities (CDSIMER Hospital)
- **Dr. Chandramma Dayananda Sagar Institute of Medical Education & Research (CDSIMER):** Constituent medical college and multi-specialty tertiary care teaching hospital located at the Harohalli Main Campus.
- Equipped with diagnostic laboratories, ICUs, 24/7 emergency care, and clinical departments supporting both student/community healthcare and MBBS/MD/MS training.

## 5. Innovation Labs & AIC-DSU Incubation
- State-of-the-art Innovation Labs and incubation spaces (AIC-DSU) spanning tens of thousands of square feet.
- Industry-partnered labs with **NVIDIA, Bosch, IBM, Cisco, Autodesk, and Dassault Systemes** supporting AI, Robotics, Data Science, IoT, and interdisciplinary research.
