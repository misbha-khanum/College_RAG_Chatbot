# Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) - Placements & Career Services
Source URL: https://www.dsu.edu.in/placements/placements-contact-us

## 1. Placement Highlights & Training
- **Highest Package:** Recent placement cycles report peak offers up to **INR 33 LPA - 35 LPA** (with top tech offers such as PhonePe at ~INR 22.5 LPA).
- **Average & Median Packages:** Average package for engineering and prominent streams is around **INR 6.2 LPA**, with NIRF-reported median packages around **INR 4.6 LPA - 5 LPA**.
- **Center for Innovation & Leadership (CIL):** Dedicated Training & Placement Cell handling pre-placement training, technical modules, soft skills, resume reviews, and mock interviews.

## 2. Major Recruiters
DSU connects with **500+ recruiting organizations** across IT, core engineering, finance, consulting, and healthcare:
- **Technology & IT:** Accenture, Infosys, Capgemini, IBM, Oracle, HCL, PhonePe, Automation Anywhere, Happiest Minds.
- **Core, Finance & Management:** EY, PwC, Cargill, ICICI Bank, Cipla, Airbus, Thoucentric, Societe Generale.
